3D Model · STL · FDM Print

3D Printed
Model

A hidden compartment model designed and sliced in Fusion 360 and Cura 5 — boat-shaped, secretly functional.

This is my final CAD project — a secret storage container modelled in Fusion 360 from scratch. The boat-shaped shell conceals an internal compartment with fitted sections, designed to hold small items discreetly. The model was exported as an STL and sliced in Ultimaker Cura 5, with print settings optimised for a clean finish in PLA. What looks like a decorative object is actually a fully functional hidden container.
