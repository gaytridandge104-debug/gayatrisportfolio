DAY 01
Introduction

Explored the core principles of UI/UX design — visual hierarchy, color theory, typography, and layout. Learned how even minor design choices influence usability and the overall user experience.

DAY 02
Explored Figma

Learned how to create frames and structure layouts in Figma. Understood how frames help organize content, maintain consistency, and build a strong foundation for UI design.

Worked on a real-world design project based on the Red Bull brand. Applied UI/UX principles to create a visually engaging and user-friendly interface, focusing on layout, consistency, and overall user experience.

Learned how to design user interfaces by applying layout, color, and typography principles. Understood how to create visually appealing and user-friendly designs that enhance the overall experience.
