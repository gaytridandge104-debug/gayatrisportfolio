Learning Modules — 01 through 07

The Journey

A complete 7-day module covering laser cutting fundamentals, machine operation, design principles, and practical fabrication skills.

DAY 01
Introduction to Laser Cutting

Learned the basics of laser cutting, different types of laser cutters, and their uses. Also learned about machine safety, focusing the laser, and setting power and speed for cutting.

Concepts & Overview 01
DAY 02
Laser Cutting

Learned how to set up the laser cutter and use the DXF file for cutting. Practiced adjusting power and speed settings and safely operating the machine.

Hardware & Safety 02
DAY 03
Materials & Compatibility

Explored the properties and characteristics of various materials used in laser cutting, including Acrylic, MDF, Plywood, Cardboard, Paper, Leather, Fabric, and Rubber. Understood how material properties affect cutting outcomes and why some materials (PVC, Vinyl) are not suitable for laser cutting.

Laser Cut
Lamp Project

A complete lamp design showcasing precision laser cutting and fabrication. This project combines digital design with precise material selection, combining aesthetics with functional engineering.

The design was created using vector graphics software, optimized for laser cutting with precise kerf compensation, and executed on a CO₂ laser cutter with acrylic and wood materials to create a modern, beautiful lighting solution.
