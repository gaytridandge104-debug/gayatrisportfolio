FabLab Student Agreement — SSGMCE

FabLab SSGMCE is responsible for

  • Teaching principles and practices of digital fabrication
  • Arranging lectures, recitations, meetings, and events
  • Evaluating and providing feedback on student work
  • Offering clear standards for completing assignments
  • Certifying and archiving student progress
  • Supervising class preparation
  • Reviewing prospective students, instructors, and labs
  • Providing central staff and infrastructure
  • Fund-raising for costs not covered by student tuition
  • Managing and reporting on finances, results, and impacts

I, as a FabLab student, am responsible for

  • Attending class lectures and participating in reviews
  • Developing and documenting assigned projects to demonstrate skills
  • Honestly reporting on my work and attributing others' work
  • Working safely
  • Leaving workspaces in the same or better condition than found
  • Participating in the upkeep of my lab
  • Ensuring my tuition to cover class costs is covered
  • Following locally applicable health and safety guidance
  • Promoting a respectful environment free of harassment and discrimination
